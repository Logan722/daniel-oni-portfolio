I'm Daniel — a cybersecurity graduate student at SMU, and this is where I'll be writing about the work behind the résumé.

Most of what I do day to day doesn't fit neatly into a bullet point. Standing up a lab, chasing down why a detection didn't fire, reading through a protocol's smart contracts — the interesting part is the process, not the one-line summary. This blog is where I plan to show that process.

## What I'll write about

A few threads I expect to keep coming back to:

- **Detection and SOC work** — I'm building a home lab to generate real attack telemetry and practice writing detections against it. Expect build notes, the things that broke, and what I learned fixing them.
- **Web3 security** — My externship at Webacy got me into smart-contract risk and on-chain analysis, and I want to keep going deeper there.
- **Grad school, in public** — Projects, coursework, and the occasional "I had no idea this was how that worked" moment.

## Why bother writing it down

Two reasons. First, writing forces me to actually understand something instead of just getting it working. Second, the security community taught me most of what I know through people who shared their notes — this is me starting to give some of that back.
